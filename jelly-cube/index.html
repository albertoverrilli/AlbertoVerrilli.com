Before building

  1. Slump. A 2.5 m gelatin cube is unlikely to hold its shape unsupported. Plan for a clear acrylic box, agar or silicone, or a cast shell.
  2. Breathing. At about 0.8 m depth a passive snorkel won't work. Use a positive-pressure regulator with constant monitoring.
  3. Cold. Gelatin sets near 10 °C or below, so hypothermia is a real risk.
  4. Release. The occupant must be freed in seconds. Use a split mould or a hollow pocket.
  5. Floor load. Over 16 t on a 7.8 m² footprint.
